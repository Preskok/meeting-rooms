Book a meeting room at Preskok

Our rooms in Ljubljana can be booked from any calendar that sends meeting invitations: Outlook, Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or anything else. Each room has its own email address and answers bookings on its own.

How booking works

  1. Check it's free

    Open the room's availability page. It shows when the room is busy, without any meeting details.

  2. Invite the room

    Create the meeting in your own calendar and add the room's email address as an attendee, the same way you'd invite a person.

  3. Get the reply

    The room answers within a few minutes. An acceptance means it's yours for that time.

After you've booked

If the room declines

It's already taken at that time, or the date is more than 180 days away. Check its availability and try another time or room.

Changing the time

Edit the meeting in your calendar and send the update. The room checks the new time and replies again.

Cancelling

Cancel the meeting and send the cancellation. The room is released straight away for someone else.

One room per meetingEvery room you add to an invitation gets booked. If you added one by mistake, remove it and send an update, otherwise it stays blocked for nobody.

Keeping a room in your calendar

The Subscribe link next to each room adds its busy times to your own calendar app. Calendar apps refresh subscriptions on their own schedule, sometimes only once a day, so check the availability page before you book.
