Booking a meeting room at Preskok

Our meeting rooms in Ljubljana can be booked from any calendar app that sends meeting invitations — Outlook, Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or anything else. Each room has its own email address and replies to bookings automatically.

How it works

  1. Check availability

    Open the room's availability link below. It shows when the room is busy, without any meeting details.

  2. Invite the room

    Create the meeting in your own calendar and add the room's email address as an attendee, just as you would invite a person.

  3. Wait for the reply

    The room answers within a few minutes. An acceptance means the room is yours for that time.

Rooms

After you've booked

If the room declines

The room is already taken at that time, or the meeting is more than 180 days away. Check the availability link and try another time or another room.

Changing the time

Edit the meeting in your calendar and send the update. The room checks the new time and replies again, so look out for that reply.

Cancelling

Cancel the meeting in your calendar and send the cancellation. The room is released straight away so someone else can use it.

Book one room per meeting. Every room you add to an invitation gets booked. If you added a room by mistake, remove it and send an update, otherwise it stays blocked for nobody.

Adding a room to your calendar

Each room also has a subscribe link that adds its busy times to your own calendar app. Calendar apps refresh subscribed calendars on their own schedule, sometimes only once a day, so always check the availability link before you book.
